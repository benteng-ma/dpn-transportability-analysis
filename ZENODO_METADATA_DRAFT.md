## Description

This archive contains the frozen analysis code, environment specification, sample metadata, selected processed derivatives, numerical result tables, main figures, and traceability audits for the manuscript “Cross-tissue projection of human sensory-ganglion transcriptomic programs in diabetic neuropathy: a multi-cohort computational study.” Human dorsal-root-ganglion programs were frozen before target analysis and projected independently across human neural tissues, rodent sensory tissues, ocular tissues, blood, and tears. No expression matrices or study-level effects were pooled across cohorts. Third-party raw data are not redistributed; stable source links, expected paths, and checksums of the analysed copies are provided.
